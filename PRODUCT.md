# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using the Kino web app to track films and series, browse profiles, and read or manage review activity.

## Product Purpose

Kino helps people keep a personal movie and TV diary, discover titles, follow other users, and share reviews across web and mobile.

## Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, reliable.

## Anti-references

Generic social feed clutter, cramped card layouts, and UI that makes reading reviews feel difficult on smaller screens.

## Design Principles

- Keep media content readable before decorative.
- Preserve a calm, focused browsing experience.
- Make responsive layouts behave predictably at each breakpoint.
- Favor content clarity over density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for strong contrast, keyboard support, readable touch targets, and responsive layouts that remain usable on narrow mobile screens.
