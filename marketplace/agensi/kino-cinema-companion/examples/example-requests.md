# Example requests

## Connected Kino

1. “Add Interstellar to my Kino watchlist.”
2. “Add The Odyssey to my Must Binge watchlist.”
3. “Mark Dune: Part Two as watched and rate it 4.5 stars.”
4. “Create a private watchlist called Sci-Fi Essentials.”
5. “Create a public watchlist called New Year's Goals with description 'living the life'.”
6. “Mark the first five episodes of Severance as watched.”
7. “Mark my next episode of The Mentalist as watched and rate it 4 stars.”
8. “Show my latest Kino diary entries.”
9. “Recommend something based on my Kino viewing history.”

Resolve Interstellar before using the configured default. Present possible
Odyssey matches and wait for a choice. Use null dates for the implicit date in
the movie and episode requests. Read actual diary and recommendation records.
For display, use the connected results template and only the returned data.

## Standalone
1. “I rated Arrival and Her 5/5, Moon and Ex Machina 4.5/5, and The Truman Show 4/5. I want a thoughtful, slightly tense film under two hours. Suggest three unwatched options and explain your evidence.”
2. “Here is a list with duplicate titles and mixed TV shows and films. Normalize it, create a ‘quiet mysteries’ list, and flag ambiguous matches. Mark it private in the output.”
3. “I watched S1E1, S1E2, S1E3, and S1E5 of this show. Summarize progress and tell me what is next only if the episode order is known.”
4. “Analyze this exported diary. Which genres and decades do I rate highest? Do not fill in missing ratings.”
5. “Create a diary entry for a rewatch on 2026-10-30. I rated it 4/5 and wrote ‘better on a second viewing.’”
